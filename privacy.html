Privacy Policy

Last updated: 30 September 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how BullWatt collects and uses personal data when you use www.bullwatt.com, its indoor cycling features, the optional Strava connection, and the BullWatt Model Context Protocol (MCP) service (together, the “Services”). It also explains what the MCP service receives and returns.

1. Who is responsible for your data

BullWatt is operated by Charles Anssens, who is the data controller for the processing described in this policy. For privacy questions or to exercise your rights, email charles@superbarbicane.com.

2. Data we collect

Website and technical data

  • Connection and security data, such as IP address, date and time, requested URL, HTTP headers, browser or client type, operating system and server errors.
  • A randomly generated BullWatt user identifier stored in your browser and sent in the userBullWatt cookie.
  • Technical diagnostic messages associated with that identifier when the training interface reports a technical event.
  • Information you send when contacting us, such as your email address, message and any information included in it.

Data kept on your device

BullWatt uses browser storage to keep your preferences and cycling data on your device, including your language, unit system, FTP setting, athlete weight, BullWatt identifier and locally recorded activity history. This data is not sent to BullWatt merely because it is stored in your browser. It may be sent when you deliberately use a feature that requires it, such as uploading an activity to Strava or sending a technical report.

Optional Strava connection

When you choose to connect Strava, BullWatt may process:

  • your Strava access and refresh tokens and their expiry time;
  • your Strava athlete identifier and sex;
  • the start dates of recent cycling activities used to display your activity history; and
  • activity data you choose to upload, such as activity name, start time, duration, distance, trainer/device name and time-series cadence, speed, power and, when available, heart rate.

BullWatt MCP service

The MCP service does not require a BullWatt account and does not ask for your name or email address. It receives requests from the MCP client or AI assistant that you choose. Depending on the feature used, the MCP inputs are:

  • Resource requests: a catalog training identifier when a complete example is requested.
  • Training search: optional search words, minimum and maximum duration, minimum and maximum intensity, phase count and maximum result count.
  • Training validation and saving: a workout object containing an identifier, name, description, duration, units and phases. A phase contains a start time, FTP ratio and optional notes. The object may also contain a creation date and source. A save request may contain an overwrite flag.
  • Workout-generation prompt: the optional free-text request supplied by you or your AI assistant.
  • Protocol data: an MCP session identifier and the connection and security data described above.

Workout names, descriptions, sources, notes and prompt requests are free-text fields. Do not include personal, medical or other sensitive information that is not needed to create your workout. FTP ratios express relative targets; BullWatt does not need your actual FTP, heart rate, identity or raw activity telemetry to search, validate or save an MCP workout.

4. MCP outputs and who can receive them

The MCP service returns the following data to your MCP client or AI assistant:

  • BullWatt workout-format documentation, generation guidance, catalog summaries and requested catalog workouts.
  • Search results containing workout identifiers, names, descriptions, duration, phase count and calculated FTP-ratio metrics.
  • Validation results containing validity status, errors, warnings and calculated duration, phase-count and FTP-ratio metrics.
  • Save results containing either an error or a generated identifier, logical file path, launch URL and validation result.
  • A generated workflow prompt that may repeat the free-text workout request provided to the prompt feature.

Your chosen MCP client or AI provider receives these outputs and determines how it processes them under its own terms and privacy policy. For example, this may be OpenAI when you connect BullWatt to ChatGPT or Anthropic when you connect it to Claude. BullWatt does not send MCP requests to an AI provider by itself and does not use MCP inputs or saved workouts to train an AI model.

A saved workout is stored under a cryptographically random identifier. Anyone who has its launch URL can retrieve the workout file, so you should treat the URL as a sharing link and avoid putting personal or sensitive information in the workout.

5. Other recipients

We disclose data only as needed to the following recipients:

  • BullWatt's hosting and infrastructure providers, which process website, stored workout, session and technical data on our behalf.
  • Strava, only when you connect your account or ask BullWatt to retrieve or upload Strava data. Strava processes that data under its privacy policy.
  • Content-delivery and embedded-content providers used by some pages. Loading their resources may disclose your IP address, browser information and the page requested to them.
  • Public authorities or professional advisers when disclosure is required by law or necessary to establish, exercise or defend legal claims.
  • A successor involved in a merger, acquisition or transfer of the service, subject to applicable confidentiality and data-protection requirements.

We do not sell personal data and do not share it for cross-context behavioural advertising.

6. Retention

Browser storage and the BullWatt identifier cookie Until you clear them in your browser. The identifier cookie is configured with a long-term expiry.
Strava tokens and athlete connection data Until you disconnect Strava, at which point BullWatt deletes its connection file. Strava may retain data under its own policy.

Web-server security and access logs According to the hosting provider's rotation period and no longer than 12 months, unless a longer period is needed to investigate an incident or comply with law.
Privacy requests and other correspondence For the time needed to respond, then up to three years to document the request or protect legal rights, unless law requires a different period.

7. Your choices and controls

  • MCP control: you choose whether your AI assistant may call BullWatt tools. You can disconnect or remove the BullWatt MCP integration in your client.
  • Saving control: searching and validating do not create a permanent workout file. A file is created only when save_training is called.
  • Deletion: because saved workouts use random identifiers and no account, keep the generated URL if you may later ask us to locate and delete a workout.
  • Strava control: connecting Strava is optional. You can disconnect it using the BullWatt logout control or revoke BullWatt in your Strava settings.
  • Device control: you can inspect or clear BullWatt cookies and local storage using your browser settings. Clearing them may reset preferences, local activities and the identifier used to find related server-side records.
  • Sensitive data: do not enter sensitive personal data in free-text MCP workout fields or technical reports.

8. Your data-protection rights

Depending on your location and the applicable law, you may have rights to access, correct or delete your personal data; restrict or object to processing; receive data you provided in a portable format; and withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. Withdrawal does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

To exercise a right, email charles@superbarbicane.com. Please provide the information needed to locate the data, such as the saved-workout URL, BullWatt identifier or Strava athlete identifier. We may ask for reasonable proof of identity and will respond within the period required by applicable law.

You may also lodge a complaint with your local data-protection authority. In France, this is the Commission Nationale de l'Informatique et des Libertés (CNIL).

9. Cookies, local storage and external content

BullWatt uses a functional identifier cookie and local storage for the purposes described above. These technologies are not used by BullWatt for behavioural advertising. You can block or delete them in your browser, although some settings, activity history, technical support and Strava features may no longer work as expected.

Some pages load libraries, fonts, images or embedded media from third parties. Your browser connects directly to those providers, which may receive connection data and set their own storage technologies. You can prevent these requests with browser privacy or content-blocking controls.

10. International data transfers

Some service providers, your chosen AI/MCP provider or Strava may process data outside your country, including outside the European Economic Area. Their own privacy notices describe the locations and transfer safeguards they use. Where BullWatt appoints a provider to process personal data outside the EEA, it will use a lawful transfer mechanism where required, such as an adequacy decision or approved standard contractual clauses.

11. Security

BullWatt uses reasonable technical and organisational safeguards designed to protect data, including validation of MCP workout data, fixed storage locations, randomly generated saved-workout identifiers and limited MCP session lifetime. No internet transmission or storage system is completely secure. A random workout link is not a substitute for access control; do not share it with people who should not access its contents.

12. Automated processing and health notice

MCP workout validation and metrics are deterministic technical checks. BullWatt does not use MCP data for automated decisions that produce legal or similarly significant effects, and the MCP server itself does not generate the workout with an AI model. Workout content and validation are not medical advice and do not assess whether a workout is suitable for your health or fitness.

13. Children's privacy

The Services are not directed to children under 18, and BullWatt does not knowingly collect their personal data. If you believe a child has provided personal data, contact us so that we can take appropriate action.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Services or our data practices change. We will publish the updated version on this page and change the “Last updated” date. Material changes will be highlighted where reasonably possible.
